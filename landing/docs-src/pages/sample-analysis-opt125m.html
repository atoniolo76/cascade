Architecture compatibility

Trace binary version is 90; the stream contains Hopper-specific kernels, including `FlashAttnFwdSm90` and SM90 GEMMs. H100 and H200 are the valid registered native targets. Older configs are rejected by the Sail app's architecture gate; preliminary pre-Sail probes also failed (for example, A100 hit the specialized-unit scheduler assertion). These are **not valid A100 predictions**. A real A100 run must choose native kernels and collect its own SM80 traces and unprofiled baseline.

The pinned and checked upstream-dev trees have no Blackwell/SM100/B200/B100/ GB200 configuration. H200 is the only shipped Hopper variant beyond H100. No H100-number-edited approximation was created. See the sweep inventory for all 12 tested and nine deprecated config files and their SM/clock evidence.
